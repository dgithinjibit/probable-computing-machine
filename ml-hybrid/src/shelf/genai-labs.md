# Startup School Gen AI labs (weeks 1-6)

_Six 2-page PDFs in `~/Desktop/ML`: `ssgenai_labs_week1.pdf` through
`ssgenai_labs_week6.pdf`. Each is titled "Startup School: Gen AI - list of covered
and recommended labs" and carries the class topic, the labs run in class, the
notebooks covered, and the steps to redeem credits on Cloud Skills Boost._

They are a syllabus, not a textbook - which is exactly why they sit on the shelf
instead of the spine. Use them as the practical half of the GenAI days.

## Mapping the six weeks onto the spine

| Lab week | Course days it follows | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Days 13-14 (how LLMs work, retrieval) | Run one covered lab while the concepts are still fresh in the same week. |
| Week 2 | Days 34-36 (trained not programmed, ELIZA, capability economics) | Before the lab, write the one sentence you would defend about where capability comes from. |
| Week 3 | Days 38-39 (21-word prompts, prompt chains) | Do the lab's prompt exercise twice: your usual style, then under 25 words. |
| Week 4 | Days 41-43 (first network, CNN, RNN) | The lab's notebook is the runnable version of the day's page. Prefer the notebook over re-reading. |
| Week 5 | Days 46-48 (on-device definitions, one-feature model, TFLite) | Do not install anything heavy here. Note which labs assume hardware you do not have. |
| Week 6 | Days 52-59 (AGI theory second pass) | Use the last week for writing, not tooling: one page, one claim, one falsifier. |

## On the credit redemption steps

The PDFs open with instructions for redeeming Cloud Skills Boost credits. Those are
hosted, metered services. This course is built to run on a 3.7GB laptop with no
installs, so treat cloud labs as optional enrichment: do them only when a lab is
genuinely impossible locally, and record in your log what you spent to find that out.
