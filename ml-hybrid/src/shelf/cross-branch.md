## The 20 images

Nineteen 480x640 phone captures and one 1024x1536 diagram. They are not random
screenshots: the deck is the cover plus pages 1-14 of a fifteen-page
"Mathematics for Machine Learning" infographic series (linear algebra, probability
and statistics, calculus for ML, optimisation, PCA and eigenvalues) by
`@code.withnarayan`, and the last page of that series is missing from the folder.
Alongside it are a Stanford LLM curriculum map, a Python library cheat-sheet card, an
HTML5 document-structure card, an ethical-hacker skills poster, and
`Neuro-Symbol.png`, the neuro-symbolic Web4 architecture diagram that belongs with
Day 54.

The whole deck has been read and transcribed into
`src/visuals/ml-cards.md` (9,648 words, one block per image: what it is, the text on
it, and which course topic it supports). That file and the images are **local only** —
neither is pushed, because they are somebody else's artwork and this repo publishes no
third-party text or images. Open the transcription directly when a day's maths feels
thin; the math series maps onto Days 1-8 and 12, and the two outliers (the ethical-hacker
poster and the HTML5 card) belong to the hacking branch rather than this one.

## What is deliberately *not* in the spine

- The cheatsheets and the 290-page Python notes: reference, not reading.
- The two worksheets: about how you study, not what you study.
- The lab lists: a syllabus for practicals that follow the GenAI days.
- The image deck: transcription and pointing, not pages of a book.

That is the whole accounting rule for this course. Every file in `~/Desktop/ML` is
either a scheduled day, a shelf reference, or a named leftover - and a leftover that
is not used is still a leftover that is named.
