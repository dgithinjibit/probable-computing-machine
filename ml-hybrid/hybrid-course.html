ML Hybrid Course

One ordered path built from the ML books on this laptop. Each day names the exact book and pages, gives you the author's own words at bite size, sends you to a local exercise, and re-tests the ideas you already completed at 1, 3, 7 and 21 lessons back.

You never restart a book. The day tells you where you are; the recall block tells you what you are keeping. If a memory goes blank, press “It went blank” and that day joins your retry queue.
