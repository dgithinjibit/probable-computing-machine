# Hacking Hybrid Course

Twelve ordered days through the security books on this laptop, each naming the exact pages, carrying a bite of the author's words, and ending at a runnable local exercise. Defensive scope only: you are learning to find and fix weaknesses, with authorization, on systems you own or are permitted to test.

## How this course works

Same rhythm as the ML branch: **recall** → **read the author** → **practice** (run the exercise, predict output first) → **check**. Review days re-test earlier ideas at 1, 3, 7 and 21 lessons back. Nothing here authorizes testing a system you do not own or have written permission to test.

## Spine order

1. _The Basics of Hacking and Penetration Testing_ — Wilhelm & Engebretson, 3rd ed. (Elsevier, 2026). See [map](maps/the-basics-of-hacking-and-p-z-library-sk-1lib-sk-z-lib-sk.md).
2. _CEH Study Guide_ — Matt Walker. See [map](maps/certified-ethical-hacker-ceh-study-guide-matt-walker.md).
3. _The Psychology of Cybersecurity_ — z-lib copy. See [map](maps/the-psychology-of-cybersecu-z-library-sk-1lib-sk-z-lib-sk.md).
4. _Kali Linux Penetration Testing Bible_ — Roberto Ghibaut Llorente et al.. See [map](maps/kali-linux-penetration-testing-bible.md).
