Hacking Hybrid Course

Twelve ordered days through the security books on this laptop. Each day names the exact book and pages, gives the author's own words at bite size, sends you to a runnable local exercise, and re-tests the ideas you already completed at 1, 3, 7 and 21 lessons back.

Defensive scope only. Everything here is about finding and fixing weaknesses with authorization, on systems you own or are explicitly permitted to assess. The exercises are synthetic and offline: no scanning, no exploits, no credential collection. Being able to explain an attack is not permission to run one.
