Blockchain Hybrid Course

Fourteen ordered days through the blockchain books on this laptop and the four code courses beside them: Mastering Blockchain, Bitcoin Programming, the Princeton crypto chapter, the Ethereum and Solidity reference, then Sui Move, Scrypto, Cairo and starklings. Each day names the exact pages, carries a bite of the author's words, and ends at a file you can actually open.

Nothing here needs the internet or a wallet. The chain toolchains are not installed on this machine, so the days that would require them ask you to read real source and write down what you could not verify instead. Do not move funds or generate keys to follow this course.
